How to read an edge

Working at the same company at the same time is a proxy for knowing someone, not proof. That is why the tiers exist.

Confirmed

Fund-to-partner edges, and a partner's own current board or advisory seats. Nothing here is inferred.

0 edges

Strong likely

Long, senior overlap on the same side of the org. Stays likely knows until a real connection record replaces it.

0 edges

Possible

Shorter windows, bigger orgs, adjacent functions. Check before anyone leans on it.

0 edges

How an edge is scored

  • 35% Overlap length: years together, full marks at four or more
  • 20% Seniority proximity: same band 1.0, one apart 0.6, further 0.2
  • 15% Function match: shared function words in the two titles
  • 15% Recency: full inside five years, fading to 0.2 at twenty-five
  • 15% Company size, inverted: under 500 people 1.0, over 10,000 0.2

0.6 and up is strong likely, 0.3 to 0.6 is possible, under 0.3 the edge is deleted. A route's strength is the product of its edges, so a short weak route loses to a longer strong one.
